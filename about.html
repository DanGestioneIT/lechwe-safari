About Lechwe Safari

About Us

Uganda's Premier Safari Specialists

Lechwe Safari is a professional safari and travel company dedicated to creating unforgettable wildlife, adventure, and cultural experiences across East Africa. We pride ourselves on offering personalised itineraries, exceptional customer service, and ethically responsible tourism practices that support conservation and local communities.

Founded in 2024, Lechwe Safari was established with a passion to showcase Africa's rich biodiversity, breathtaking landscapes, and unique cultures. We have grown into a trusted safari operator known for reliability, transparency, and memorable outdoor experiences.
