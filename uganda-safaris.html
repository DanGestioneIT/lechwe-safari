The Pearl of Africa

Unmatched Uganda Safaris with Lechwe Safari

Uganda earns its name "The Pearl of Africa" in the most natural way. Massively endowed with gorilla mountains, elephant herds, thousands of bird species — Uganda's diversity is absolutely incredible.

From snowcapped Rwenzori mountains, to vast Lake Victoria, the lush impenetrable rainforests of Kibale and Bwindi, along with endless plain savannahs — Uganda is exceptional. Wildlife safaris here are completely astonishing. With Lechwe Safari, travelers don't just see wildlife — they feel like they are part of it. Professional tour guides, expert knowledge of Uganda's top tourism hubs, and personal service from arrival to departure.

Uganda's Top Parks

Where We Take You

🦍 Bwindi Impenetrable Forest

UNESCO World Heritage Site, home to nearly half of all remaining mountain gorillas.

🐒 Kibale National Park

The world's best chimpanzee trekking destination with over 1,500 chimps.

🦁 Queen Elizabeth NP

Tree-climbing lions in Ishasha, Kazinga Channel boat cruises, and excellent Big 4 game drives.

💦 Murchison Falls NP

Uganda's largest park. The Nile's most dramatic spectacle plus superb Nile cruise wildlife viewing.
